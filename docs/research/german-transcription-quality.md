# German local transcription: model evidence and reproducible evaluation

Research date and source access date: **2026-10-10**. Planning artifact for [comentator/tdesktop issue #3](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/3), under the user-confirmed [wayfinder map #1](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/1). Research branch: `research/german-transcription-quality`. Intended branch-relative finding link: [`docs/research/german-transcription-quality.md`](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/blob/research/german-transcription-quality/docs/research/german-transcription-quality.md); this is a publication target, **not a claim that the branch has been pushed**.

## Scope and answer

Evaluate **Whisper large-v3 and large-v3-turbo**, **Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3**, **Canary 1B v2**, and two compact controls, **multilingual Whisper small** and **Canary 180M Flash**. Include **Qwen3-ASR 1.7B** as a newer, German-capable challenger whose German-specific quality remains unestablished by the consulted evidence; its **0.6B** sibling is an optional compact challenger. A German Whisper adaptation can be an additional research comparison, but its author-reported gains do not establish a product advantage, training/test independence, or preserved multilingual capabilities. These are evaluation candidates, **not a model selection or approval to download**.

Accuracy in German comes first. A Transcript means text in the spoken language, not translation, for a manually requested **Local transcription** of a **Voice message** or **Video message** (circle). The constraints remain strictly on-device, no Premium requirement, approved model downloads plus local import, multilingual automatic language detection and language override, and local transcript persistence. This note does not design storage, UI, model delivery, or a backend. All desktop platforms remain in scope; eventual validation starts on macOS. A Python/CUDA example or multilingual model card does **not** establish macOS, Windows, or Linux product compatibility. The peer runtime research owns backend and platform feasibility.

**Research performed:** primary papers, model/data cards, publisher repositories, license pages, and model-repository JSON metadata. **Not performed:** model or audio downloads, model conversion, local inference, benchmark execution, builds, tests, lint, or formatting. No measurements below were generated locally. Metadata retrieval is not inspection of the weight contents.

### Evidence notation

- **Published measurement:** a number reported in a named paper, card, or evaluation artifact, with its dataset and limitations. Publication does not make it independent.
- **Author claim:** capability, robustness, speed, or quality stated by the model/data publisher without an applicable independent measurement here.
- **Shared evaluation:** multiple models evaluated with a common published protocol; distinguish a model-author-involved evaluation from an independent one.
- **[INFERENCE]:** interpretation or a proposed evaluation choice, not an observed result.
- **Unknown:** not established by the sources consulted, not evidence of absence.

## 1. What German evidence actually establishes

### 1.1 Shared standard-speech evidence, not a desktop-message winner

The [Open ASR Leaderboard paper, version 1, 2025-10-08, Table 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06961v1#S2.T4) provides a primary shared multilingual comparison. Its **German average WER** is **4.97% for Whisper large-v3, 4.90% for Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, and 4.96% for Canary 1B v2**. German uses **FLEURS and CoVoST-2**, not German MLS: the paper's dataset table marks MLS for French/Italian/Spanish/Portuguese. These are reported language-level averages, not WER for private Voice messages, nor a pooled score invented here. Evaluation used an NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB, CUDA 12.6, and batch size 64 when memory allowed, reduced as necessary. The [public evaluation repository](https://github.com/huggingface/open_asr_leaderboard) exposes the evaluation approach.

This is more useful than comparing unrelated cards, but **not fully independent of the compared model publishers**: the paper includes NVIDIA authors and a Mistral author alongside Hugging Face/Cambridge authors. Its tiny differences lack a reported German paired confidence interval in that table. The paper describes normalization including English-specific operations; a future German reproduction must inspect the exact multilingual evaluator revision rather than silently apply English number rules. The shared paper does not give an equivalent German result for turbo, the compact controls, the German adaptation, or Qwen3-ASR. It does not test the target desktop runtimes/quantizations or a representative consented message corpus. [Source: paper, affiliations, §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06961v1).

**Reserve candidates must not be hidden by the initial shortlist.** The same [Table 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06961v1#S2.T4) gives **Microsoft Phi-4 Multimodal Instruct 4.50% German average WER**, the best German point estimate in that table, and **Mistral Voxtral Mini 3B 5.36%**. Those are shared-protocol published results with the same independence/domain/uncertainty limitations, not proof that a dedicated ASR model must win.

- [`microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct`](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct) is a **5.6B** multimodal model with **MIT** weight/repository terms ([license](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct/blob/main/LICENSE)); its **eight audio languages include German**, distinct from its broader text-language list. The card documents a local transcription instruction, “Transcribe the audio clip into text,” and separate translation instructions. A dedicated automatic-LID result/probability interface or a hard source-language override equivalent to Whisper's decoder option is **not established** by that card; prompting for German is not verified hard override support. Its example uses `trust_remote_code=True`, requiring pinned review of repository code, not arbitrary local-import execution. At [revision `93f923e1a7727d1c4f446756212d9d3e8fcc5d81`](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct/tree/93f923e1a7727d1c4f446756212d9d3e8fcc5d81), [publisher API metadata](https://huggingface.co/api/models/microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct?blobs=true) reports 5,574,460,384 BF16 stored main-model parameters; the three main Safetensors shards total **11,149,227,208 bytes**, with separate **922,782,296-byte speech adapter** and **738,232,904-byte vision adapter** files. These are separate components, not a measured transcription RAM budget.
- [`mistralai/Voxtral-Mini-3B-2507`](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Voxtral-Mini-3B-2507) is the exact **Mini 1.0 / 2507** release, not Small 24B or a later transcription variant. Its card declares **Apache-2.0** weights, German among **eight languages**, **dedicated transcription mode**, automatic source-language prediction by default, and an explicit transcription-request `language` field; the demonstrated `language="en"` establishes a language-control API, while forced-German behavior still needs runtime evaluation. Audio-understanding/chat mode is a different task and must not substitute for transcription. At [revision `3060fe34b35ba5d44202ce9ff3c097642914f8f3`](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Voxtral-Mini-3B-2507/tree/3060fe34b35ba5d44202ce9ff3c097642914f8f3), [publisher API metadata](https://huggingface.co/api/models/mistralai/Voxtral-Mini-3B-2507?blobs=true) reports **4,676,271,104 stored parameters** (mostly BF16, some FP32), distinct from the 3B backbone/variant label; `consolidated.safetensors` is **9,348,806,528 bytes**, while the alternative two Transformers shards total **9,356,474,312 bytes**. These are alternative distributions, not files to add together into a required download.

**[INFERENCE]** Keep both as explicit **reserve accuracy challengers**, with Phi particularly important because of its better shared German point estimate. The initial transcription-focused campaign concentrates on conventional ASR controls and the named newer ASR candidates; broader instruction-conditioned/model-component interfaces need a verified offline transcription-only seam and language-control contract. This is experiment sequencing, **not an evidence-based rejection, a resource-budget exclusion, or an assertion that Voxtral lacks transcription mode**. Promote either into the campaign when peer runtime research establishes that seam, and before any final choice claiming best German accuracy. Transformers/vLLM/runtime licenses remain separate from these weight licenses; desktop compatibility, German-message fidelity, hallucination/refusal/paraphrase behavior, and exact runtime precision remain unmeasured here.

**Conclusion:** common-protocol German evidence exists for a subset, and independent German dialect evidence exists below. **No fully independent apples-to-apples German result covering the entire proposed shortlist and the target message conditions was established by this research.** Do not merge these different evaluations into a global ranking.

### 1.3 Independent German dialect evidence and the reference-policy problem

[Blaschke et al., Betthupferl, 2025, §§4.1–4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.02894v1#S4) independently compare multilingual Whisper sizes, German fine-tunes, a Swiss German turbo fine-tune, XLS-R, and MMS in a common setup. Their corpus has about four hours of Franconian/Bavarian/Alemannic read speech and half an hour of Standard German. Language is forced to German. They ignore case/punctuation, tokenize words by whitespace, and report **mean sentence-level** scores rather than our proposed pooled corpus WER.

Whisper large-v3 is the strongest model **among the variants tested in that study**. The paper reports **31% WER / 16% CER** for dialect audio against **Standard German translated references**, versus **9% WER / 3% CER** for Standard German audio/references. Large-v3-turbo has a very minor decline, without an exact number transcribed here from its plotted figure. NVIDIA v3/v2 and Qwen3-ASR are not part of that experiment. Larger Whisper models outperform smaller ones there. Fine-tuning outcomes are mixed: only the largest Standard German fine-tune clearly improves dialect-to-standard performance; the Swiss German fine-tune generally worsens results, apart from a limited Swabian dialect-ASR improvement. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.02894v1).

**Important boundary:** the 31% result measures dialect-to-Standard-German **speech translation/normalization**, not purely spoken-language literal transcription. Dialect and Standard German references differ lexically, grammatically, and in word boundaries. Valid alternative renderings can be counted as WER errors. The study also reports only moderate correlation between automatic metrics and human meaning/fluency ratings. Its [released code, references, and predictions](https://github.com/mainlp/betthupferl) are useful evaluation evidence; audio requires **case-by-case permission from Bayerischer Rundfunk**, so it is not an openly redistributable audio benchmark. [Paper, footnote 1 and §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.02894v1).

[INFERENCE] Dialect preservation versus rendering in Standard German is a **reference-policy decision**, not something to hide in normalization. For this product's Transcript definition, evaluate spoken-language fidelity first, with dialect-specific human adjudication and an optional separate Standard German readability reference. Do not introduce translation as an unasked product behavior.

### 1.4 German adaptations: verified release labels, limited evidential confidence

The publisher's [`primeline/whisper-large-v3-turbo-german` card](https://huggingface.co/primeline/whisper-large-v3-turbo-german) declares Apache-2.0, German, 809M parameters, a base relation to `primeline/whisper-large-v3-german`, public Safetensors distribution, and an evaluation table with **3.649% overall WER for OpenAI turbo**, **3.279% for OpenAI large-v3**, **2.734% for primeline large-v3 German**, and **2.628% for primeline turbo German** across TUDA-De, Common Voice 19, and MLS. These are **author-run** shared results, not independent validation.

The linked [evaluation artifact](https://huggingface.co/datasets/flozi00/asr-german-mixed-evals) includes references/prediction data and a script using BF16, beam 1, forced `de`, `max_new_tokens=384`, and German number conversion, `ß`→`ss`, punctuation/hyphen removal, and accent transliteration. Its displayed values **differ slightly from the model card**: OpenAI large-v3 3.28, turbo 3.64, primeline turbo German 2.62, small 9.54, base 18.75. These differences are an unresolved version/protocol issue; preserve each source's numbers, do not silently average or select the favorable version. Its current script does not establish a common executable path for every extra model in its displayed table (for example, transducer entries); exact run provenance needs retrieval before reproduction.

The [`primeline/whisper-large-v3-german` card](https://huggingface.co/primeline/whisper-large-v3-german) separately reports **3.002% WER / 0.81% CER** on Common Voice 15 German, but describes its training data only generically. Its [distilled German sibling](https://huggingface.co/primeline/distil-whisper-large-v3-german) declares 756M/BF16/1.51GB and names Common Voice, MLS, and internal data, but provides no applicable independent German score there. “Minimal quality loss” remains an author claim.

The [German mixed training card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/flozi00/asr-german-mixed) says data were retranscribed/filtered using primeline Whisper large-v3, with grammar correction, and inconsistently mentions Common Voice 17 and 19. It does not provide a single verified composite license or a complete disjoint training/evaluation ID manifest. **[INFERENCE]** Publisher-assisted reference correction can favor that publisher's transcription style; the card alone cannot rule out leakage or label bias. It is not proof that contamination occurred.

**Status:** the primeline turbo German adaptation merits a **conditional German-only research arm**, because weight license declaration, downloadable artifact metadata, and German evaluation provenance are identifiable. Those verified items are not a blanket clearance of internal training data rights or local-product redistribution. Obtain exact training/test partitions, reference provenance, source-rights documentation, and independent held-out quality before approving it as a curated download. Its German-only card does not verify retained automatic language detection, non-German transcription, or mixed-language fidelity after adaptation. Do not infer those from its Whisper ancestry.

[CrisperWhisper v1's current publisher card](https://huggingface.co/nyralabs/CrisperWhisper) declares **CC-BY-NC-4.0**, German/English and large-v3 ancestry, and now marks v1 superseded. Its verbatim/filler and hallucination-mitigation descriptions are publisher claims; the primeline shared artifact gives additional German author-run evidence. The noncommercial weight restriction means it is **not in the unrestricted redistribution shortlist**. A successor is a distinct variant requiring separate evidence/license research, not a reason to carry v1's score or license forward.

## 3. Failure modes the evaluation must expose

1. **Dialects and accents:** the independent Betthupferl results above establish a substantial dialect/Standard German gap and fine-tuning tradeoffs. Accented Standard German and dialect speech are distinct slices, not one “accent” score. Swiss German is not proof of Bavarian/Swabian generalization. Common Voice's self-declared Swiss accent label is not a professionally verified dialect corpus. [Betthupferl](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.02894v1), [current German Common Voice card](https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmu5ktqhe00pgmh07p1xamabd).
2. **Noise and distance:** OpenAI reports robustness but warns of uneven accuracy; NVIDIA cards report noise experiments largely on English. Their domain/noise/accent caveats explicitly deny perfect transcripts and warn about word-for-word/incomplete sentences. German noisy message accuracy is still unknown. Synthetic noise scores cannot replace naturally recorded German messages. [OpenAI card](https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/model-card.md), [Parakeet](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3), [Canary v2](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/canary-1b-v2).
3. **Very short speech and initial silence:** automatic language evidence can be weak; Whisper's official detection uses the initial window, not all future speech. **[INFERENCE]** “Ja”, “okay”, a name, or a clipped syllable are necessary tests, but no German error rate for these is claimed here. Measure dropped first/last words, false non-speech decisions, and wrong language separately.
4. **Silence/non-speech hallucinations:** OpenAI explicitly warns that predicted text may not have been spoken and that repetition mitigation is imperfect. An independent [Barański et al. study](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.11378v1) reported nonempty letter/number hallucinations on **40.3% of 301,317 filtered/synthetic non-speech clips** using Whisper **large-v3, forced English, temperature zero, other defaults including greedy decoding**, after basic normalization/delooping. This is a laboratory stress distribution, **not German or customer-message incidence**, and not a turbo result. The study says none of its tested mitigation approaches completely protects against hallucinations. Do not adopt a phrase blacklist from that paper as a product fix or claim VAD guarantees safety.
5. **Newer model hallucination robustness:** NVIDIA's [technical report §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14128v1#S3.SS2) says it adds about 36,000 hours of empty-target non-speech training to reduce hallucinations. This is a credible training-design claim, **not a German silence-hallucination measurement**. Neither transducer architecture nor newer release date proves immunity. Qwen's robustness claims likewise need local stress evidence.
6. **Mixed languages:** per-file detection, explicit source/target prompts, unified vocabularies, and supported-language counts have different meanings. The [NVIDIA report](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14128v1) motivates a unified tokenizer partly by natural code-switching; this is not a measured German–English mixed-message success rate. Test within-sentence switches, sentences after a leading foreign word, borrowed words, and unsupported-language sections. Count unwanted translation and deletions; a fluent monolingual rewrite can be less faithful than an awkward mixed transcript.
7. **Names/numbers and disfluencies:** rare names, street addresses, dates, money, phone numbers, negatives, repairs, and fillers are poorly summarized by one WER average. German compounds/word splitting can inflate WER; semantic normalization can conceal wrong values. **[INFERENCE]** retain raw outputs and adjudicate meaningful errors, rather than preferring clean-looking but fabricated text.
8. **Chunk boundaries and video containers:** Whisper's official transcription uses sliding 30-second windows; Flash's card describes input smaller than 40 seconds and chunked long-form inference, with shorter timestamp chunks. Long-context claims on A100 are not desktop RAM guarantees. Codec loss, stream selection, truncation, and stereo downmix can alter quality independently of the model. [Whisper README](https://github.com/openai/whisper), [Flash card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/canary-180m-flash), [Parakeet card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3).

Keep a separately recruited development panel out of the held-out test. Report subgroup counts/missing metadata and avoid publishing identifiable combinations.

## 5. Reproducible, entirely offline protocol

Everything in this section is a **proposed protocol**, not an executed benchmark or implementation task.

### 5.1 Freeze the experiment and protect held-out material

1. Pre-register the shortlist/configurations, reference policy, slices, aggregation weights, primary/secondary metrics, and decision margins **before reading test-model outputs**. Public historical results guide candidate inclusion, not decoder tuning on test.
2. Define a development set and sealed held-out set, disjoint by **speaker and recording session**, and by content/book/prompt where feasible. Keep repeated microphones/codecs/augmentations of one recording in the same partition. Preserve official benchmark splits; use their validation/dev sets for tuning. Common Voice spontaneous needs a newly declared speaker-disjoint partition because its current release is unassigned.
3. Publish a contamination ledger: model training sources/cutoffs known, source overlaps known, exact IDs/hashes excluded, and remaining unknowns. FLEURS/Common Voice/MLS appear in NVIDIA training descriptions; internal/weakly labeled Whisper/Qwen data and German fine-tune mixes do not permit a complete unseen-data guarantee. Dataset-level training inclusion is **not proof of test leakage**, nor does “test split” prove cleanliness. [NVIDIA data report](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14128v1#S3), [large-v3 card](https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3), [German mix card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/flozi00/asr-german-mixed).
4. Newly recorded material after pinned releases, not shared with model authors or uploaded to services, is the strongest reachable leakage control. Do not train/fine-tune or alter references based on model outputs. Keep public-corpus and purpose-recorded results separate so training familiarity cannot masquerade as message quality.

### 5.2 Pin assets and decoding settings

For every run manifest record model repository, full commit SHA, every required file and locally verified SHA-256/byte count, original publisher/weight license, conversion commands/version/input/output hashes if any, tokenizer/processor/generation-config revisions, runtime/library/OS/CPU/GPU versions, compute precision/quantization, threads/batch size/device, and random seeds/determinism settings. Use full revisions rather than `main`, “large”, or an alias that may drift. Retain a rights/approval manifest for each staged model and dataset. **No assets have been staged by this research.**

Separate two named comparisons:

- **Research accuracy reference:** supported high-precision configurations, no silent quantization, fixed common audio/reference/scoring. Architecture-specific decoding may differ, but is fully recorded and held fixed; equal “beam=1” is not equal decoding across TDT and autoregressive models.
- **Deployment-candidate accuracy:** exact peer-approved desktop runtime/conversion/precision/device for the same held-out clips, starting macOS. Report quality delta against the reference, not an assumed equivalence. Later Windows/Linux checks are separate hardware/software cells. Latency/memory are secondary feasibility information and cannot compensate for an unmeasured German regression.

Explicitly log: source-language auto/forced setting and returned language; task `transcribe` / equal Canary source–target; sampling temperature schedule, beam size, greedy mode, token budget, timestamp mode, previous-text conditioning, prompts/hotwords (none in baseline), repetition/fallback thresholds, VAD model/hash/threshold/padding, channel selection, chunk length/overlap/context/stitching, long-form resets, and no-speech thresholds. Start without optional post-processing/VAD in the raw-model research arm where supported; if the intended engine includes VAD, report the combined pipeline **as a different arm**, including lost speech. Do not edit a blacklist to erase errors. Tune only on development, then freeze. Do not choose decoder settings from the best test score.

For Whisper, the documented default can include temperature fallback, no-speech thresholds, and previous-token conditioning; a fixed temperature-zero/beam-one research arm and the eventual production-like arm must not be conflated. For Parakeet use the supported exact greedy TDT configuration. For Canary freeze beam/prompt/punctuation/chunk settings and any separate LID component. For Qwen freeze its supported greedy/token-budget/context settings and make local input paths mandatory. An unsupported override should be logged as **unsupported**, not counted as a successful forced-language test.

### 5.3 Decode actual message media, not a handpicked WAV substitute

Freeze a canonical, audited audio decode/resample/downmix pipeline. Save the original-media hash, selected audio stream, container/codec/profile, sample rate/channels/sample count, duration, decode/resample library revision, conversion settings, canonical PCM hash, and crop/time offsets. Decode locally; no demo Spaces, cloud ASR, cloud annotation, URL audio fetching, or telemetry carrying audio/transcripts. Block network during inference/scoring and verify that absence in the eventual execution, rather than relying only on an “offline” option.

Whisper and NVIDIA research interfaces use 16 kHz audio preprocessing; use their exact documented feature extraction and record it. If another family needs a different input sample rate, derive it once from the same canonical decoded signal with a pinned resampler. Do not force the wrong preprocessing just to claim identical input. Preserve stereo originals and specify downmix to avoid phase cancellation; test the original decode and deterministic mono path.

Evaluate both **clean original recordings** and **lawfully existing, explicitly consented local exports of Telegram Voice and Video messages**, or **entirely offline encoder-equivalent message files** generated from consented recordings with pinned, verified codec/container settings. **Do not create or send evaluation recordings through Telegram/cloud services, or upload evaluation audio, video, or transcripts anywhere.** Retain actual MIME/container/codec metadata rather than assuming all files use one codec; label offline encoder-equivalent files as approximations unless equivalence is established. Include typical Voice message Opus paths and video-container audio as observed in consented existing exports or established by peer runtime research. A container/stream decode failure is an end-to-end failure, not excluded “bad audio”. Treat controlled transcoding at declared codec/bitrate/sample-rate settings as paired derivatives, not extra independent samples. Report original-versus-message and voice-versus-video deltas, first/last-word truncation, and chunk-boundary errors. Never transcribe visible lip/text information as if it came from the audio; this is audio transcription of a Video message, not multimodal text recovery.

### 5.4 Gold references and normalization

Use two independent proficient German listeners for the decisive recordings, blinded to model outputs; adjudicate disagreement, with dialect-competent reviewers for dialect slices. Annotate actual speech, language spans, intelligibility, speaker overlap, non-speech spans, and sensitive semantic slots. Predefine treatment of fillers, false starts, partial words, compounds, spelling, punctuation, and dialect orthography. Mark truly unintelligible spans consistently and report their duration; report both all-clip and explicitly masked scoring where justified instead of silently deleting difficult clips.

Retain **raw reference and raw hypothesis**. For public datasets whose only gold is already normalized, label “raw” as scoring the provided reference without added normalization; it is not newly recovered punctuation/diacritics. FLEURS has separate raw/normalized fields; MLS/TUDA/etc. need dataset-specific reference provenance noted. Do not force a publisher's grammar-corrected reference to stand in for verbatim speech.

Report a conservative German normalization in a pinned, versioned specification: Unicode NFC; consistent whitespace; case-insensitive secondary scoring; specified punctuation handling; preserve **ä/ö/ü/ß**, names, negation, fillers, language switches, and lexical identity. **Do not apply an English normalizer to German.** Avoid automatic accent stripping or `ß`→`ss` in the primary normalized score. If Swiss orthographic equivalence, number-word/digit equivalence, accepted compound splits, or alternate dialect spellings are useful, publish each as a **separate sensitivity analysis** with explicit allowed mappings. Never normalize “vierzehn” and “vierzig” together, remove meaningful words, or substitute Standard German grammar for dialect speech. The primeline evaluation's transformations are recorded evidence, not automatically the right policy here.

### 5.5 Metrics and empty-reference rules

For references with speech, compute **raw and conservatively normalized WER and CER**. WER is `(substitutions + deletions + insertions) / reference words`; CER uses reference characters with a declared whitespace/punctuation convention. Primary aggregate is **pooled corpus WER** (sum edit counts divided by sum reference words), also report mean/median per-message WER, CER, S/D/I breakdown, and speaker-macro averages. Message WER can exceed 100% and short references are unstable; do not clip to 100 or hide them in a single mean. Publish per-source results, predeclared balanced slice means, and any deployment-distribution-weighted aggregate **only if actual weights are available**, not invented from the research set.

**Empty references:** mathematical WER has denominator zero. Keep verified silence/non-speech clips out of speech-only WER and report **nonempty-output rate**, inserted words/chars per clip and per non-speech minute, duration/type slices, and content-reviewed hallucination severity. Report both literally nonempty raw output and nonempty lexical output after the declared normalization so punctuation-only output is visible. If using a scoring library convention, pin and label it: [JiWER 4.0+](https://github.com/jitsi/jiwer#a-note-on-empty-references) defines empty/empty as zero and empty/nonempty word scores as insertion counts, **not a conventional percentage**. A hallucination cannot disappear merely because the scorer rejects an empty reference. Evaluate positive speech detection too: “empty transcript” on an intelligible one-word message is a deletion failure, not successful silence handling.

Additional quality metrics:

- **Names/entities:** exact entity-span match, entity precision/recall/F1, and name/street/acronym character accuracy; define alternate accepted spellings before evaluation. Separate uncommon/foreign names. Precision detects invented entities, not just missed ones.
- **Numbers and meaning-critical slots:** exact-value accuracy for dates/times/money/phone/digit strings, plus whether the value was stated versus invented; slot precision/recall. Preserve units, decimal sign, polarity, and correction order. Score raw surface forms and independently annotated semantic values, not an unchecked automatic number normalizer.
- **Semantic fidelity:** blinded human rate of material meaning errors (negation, action, person, time/value), unsupported additions, lost clauses, and unwanted translation. Report utterance-level critical-error rate and adjudicated examples, not a cloud-LLM-derived “semantic score”.
- **Readable transcript quality:** human fidelity and readability scores separately; optional punctuation/capitalization diagnostics. Fluency does not make hallucinations acceptable. Dialect-preservation and Standard-readable rendering are separate reference axes.
- **Coverage/failures:** decode failure, timeout/resource failure, unsupported mode/language, truncation/token-budget exhaustion, empty output on speech, repeated-loop output, and untranscribed duration. All held-out message IDs remain in the report. Report a completion rate and failure counts alongside quality; successful-only WER alone is misleading.

### 5.6 Language detection and override test matrix

Run automatic mode and **forced German** on the same German held-out IDs, plus automatic/forced true language for supported non-German controls and explicit wrong overrides as stress tests. Never present deliberate wrong-language results as ordinary accuracy. For Canary, distinguish oracle known-language ASR from the proposed **local-LID→Canary** pipeline; score LID misroutes and downstream transcript errors. For Parakeet, do not assume a force option exists. For adaptations, include non-German controls to expose catastrophic forgetting.

Record language-label accuracy/confusion, supported/out-of-coverage status, abstention behavior if available, auto-minus-oracle WER/CER/semantic error, and override improvement/degradation. Only evaluate calibration if a documented meaningful probability exists. For mixed-language clips, use annotated language spans and count preservation of each span, translated/deleted foreign speech and language-specific WER; a single file language label is not sufficient ground truth. Test initial silence/foreign greeting followed by German, one-word names, and clipped words. Where no LID label is exposed, report that capability gap separately from transcript fidelity; do not fabricate a label from the transcript to claim native detection.

### 5.7 Slices, blind review, uncertainty, and release evidence

Report clip/speaker/session counts, audio minutes, reference words/chars, and duration distributions for each source, message type, language/dialect/accent, natural-noise/microphone condition, codec/bitrate, speech length, silence fraction, and name/number slice. Slice definitions may overlap; publish their overlap and avoid double-counted “overall” totals. Mark tiny subgroups as inconclusive instead of ranking them confidently.

Blind reviewers to model/runtime identity; randomize outputs within clip and order across reviewers. Have at least two reviewers rate fidelity/readability on anchored scales and flag material errors; adjudicate disagreements, record inter-rater agreement and retain disagreement examples. Do not let the development team reveal “new model” identities to evaluators. Dialect recordings need reviewers competent in the variety, not merely Standard German fluency. The [Betthupferl human study](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.02894v1#S4.SS3.SSS1) motivates complementing WER rather than assuming a perfect WER-to-meaning correspondence.

Use **paired bootstrap confidence intervals for model differences**, resampling **speakers or sessions** rather than treating repeated codecs/microphones/noise versions as independent. Declare seed, replicate count (e.g. 10,000), confidence level (e.g. 95%), aggregation statistic, and group handling in advance. For non-speech rates/critical-error proportions report appropriate intervals; when zero failures are observed, state the sample size and upper bound, not “zero risk”. Report uncertainty and multiple-comparison caution for numerous slices. No margin is statistically or product-validated by this note.

The eventual report should include exact manifests/settings, immutable asset hashes, split/rights/leakage ledger, raw model outputs under controlled access, public aggregate metrics and confidence intervals, failures, and safely redacted adjudicated error examples. Reproducibility for restricted Common Voice/broadcast/private material means **authorized users can obtain the named version and rebuild the declared split**, not that raw audio or speaker metadata must be mirrored publicly. Publish licensed code/IDs/settings as allowed; consented private assets remain local. A downloadable directory must not silently contain chat data.

## 6. Shortlist, remaining questions, and decision boundary

### Shortlist to evaluate, not a winner

**Accuracy reference pair:** Whisper **large-v3** and **large-v3-turbo**. They anchor the best-established multilingual German/dialect evidence and test whether turbo's quality loss is acceptable for actual German messages, rather than assumed from speed claims.

**Architecture challengers:** Parakeet **TDT 0.6B v3** and Canary **1B v2**. Both have direct publisher German measurements and shared German evidence close to large-v3, with public permissively licensed weights. Their detection/override differences must be treated as capability gates, and desktop runtime support remains unestablished here.

**Compact controls:** multilingual Whisper **small** and Canary **180M Flash**. Small is a conventional cross-size control; Flash has direct German CV16.1 evidence and is much smaller, but only four languages and English-by-default behavior. Whisper **base** is optional if the peer runtime evaluation needs a lower resource boundary; it is not nominated as German-accuracy-equivalent.

**Newer challenger:** Qwen3-ASR **1.7B**, with **0.6B optional** to compare a smaller sibling. German plus automatic/forced-language semantics and local distribution are verified, but consulted quality aggregates are not German-only. Treat it as **unmeasured for the precise German question**, not as a leaderboard winner; reconcile actual artifact/parameter accounting first.

**Conditional German adaptation arm:** primeline **whisper-large-v3-turbo-german** against original turbo on independently transcribed, newly consented held-out speech. The author-run evidence warrants testing a hypothesis, not curated distribution approval. Resolve provenance/split/reference and multilingual-retention questions before promotion. The large German and distilled siblings need not expand the initial campaign absent a specific unanswered accuracy/resource question. Noncommercial CrisperWhisper v1 and unverified conversions are not promoted into the unrestricted shortlist.

**Reserve accuracy challengers:** exact **Microsoft Phi-4-multimodal-instruct** and **Mistral Voxtral-Mini-3B-2507**, detailed in §1.1. Phi has the best German point estimate in the shared table; neither is dismissed by an invented memory/latency limit. Establish transcription-only local interfaces and required language-control behavior, then promote as appropriate before a final German-accuracy decision. Their broader multimodal capabilities are not permission to recover transcripts from video images or perform translation.

### Precisely unresolved

1. Which candidate has materially better **spoken-language German message fidelity**, not merely FLEURS/CoVoST or an author's label-corrected mix score, at the chosen high precision and at the exact desktop deployment precision?
2. Can the proposed engine satisfy **both auto-detection and actual language override** for Parakeet, and auto-detection for Canary, wholly locally? What is the end-to-end quality cost of any separate LID component, particularly on very short speech and code-switching?
3. What are dialect-preserving reference conventions and user-acceptable readability expectations? Standard German translated-reference WER cannot decide that implicitly.
4. What are German silence/non-speech false-output rates, rare-name/number/negation errors, codec/chunk losses, and failure rates? No consulted source establishes those target-population rates.
5. Is German adaptation genuinely better on novel independent gold, without training/evaluation overlap or publisher-reference bias, and does it retain required multilingual behavior?
6. Are model notices, publisher-license discrepancies, source provenance, and approved local package/import format resolved? Weight declarations are not blanket permission for all associated datasets/remote code.
7. Can peer-approved runtimes execute exact candidate variants on macOS first and then Windows/Linux within defined memory/latency/resource constraints? Cards and A100 benchmarks cannot answer this. Parameter/file-size label discrepancies, especially Qwen's total stored count, must be reconciled rather than turned into guessed hardware budgets.
8. Is the consented sample representative and large enough for the claimed dialect/accent/noise slices and non-inferiority margins? Public corpora and a pilot's small confidence interval alone do not prove deployment robustness.

**Decision boundary [INFERENCE]:** after an authorized offline campaign, select only among configurations meeting privacy, license/distribution, real automatic/override behavior, platform feasibility, and reliability gates. Prefer the model/pipeline with demonstrably better German semantic fidelity and predeclared WER/CER/slice results; use paired uncertainty and critical-error rates to distinguish meaningful improvements. A smaller/faster configuration becomes acceptable only if its German quality is within a **pre-agreed** non-inferiority margin with no unacceptable short-speech/dialect/name/number/silence regression. If results are inconclusive, report the tie/uncertainty and expand consented evidence, not an invented winner. Do not weaken the confirmed multilingual/platform requirements to make one model fit.

This completes documentation research only. **No final model, backend, conversion, download catalog, implementation, or benchmark outcome is selected.**

# German spontaneous and mixed-language audio sources for Whisper fidelity research

Research date: 2026-10-11. Scope: external publisher documentation, licenses, release metadata and HTTP HEAD access facts. This report supports a source-specific acquisition decision for [Qualify German fidelity and unclear-span behavior](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/12); it is not acquisition approval, a qualification result or a release threshold.

The subsequent user instruction to work without a human in the loop supersedes this report's proposed new live-review procedure. Its publisher/source facts and reference-quality warnings remain; no review is fabricated. See [the current autonomous artifact](whisper-german-fidelity.md) for the approved acquisitions, exercised controls and provisional-label policy.

## Decision summary

**A genuinely small, public, explicitly licensed acquisition choice is available, but only as a read-speech control:** four original human recordings from Thorsten-Voice's publisher repository, pinned below, totaling **484,236 bytes**. All four pinned audio URLs answered HEAD with HTTP 200, `audio/wav`, and matching `Content-Length`. No audio payload was retrieved. The publisher distinguishes these `-gt.wav` original recordings from the surrounding synthesized/vocoder samples. These four controls do **not** cover spontaneous fillers, repetitions, self-corrections, unclear spans, mixed-language speech, long recordings or dialect fidelity.

**No immediately usable, legally unambiguous, small spontaneous/mixed-language pilot was established among the investigated sources.** FOLK/DGD and Kiel offer real spontaneous speech but impose research/access/redistribution restrictions. CALLHOME German has unusually explicit disfluency/unclear-span reference conventions, but requires separately licensed audio and transcripts and restricts nonmember use. DECM is a relevant spontaneous German-English benchmark, but its public repository supplies no verified lawful audio acquisition path or complete public reference distribution. Spoken Wikipedia/Idiap is a lawful mixed-language **read-speech** alternative, not spontaneous behavior evidence, and its reference construction is normalized/automatically aligned.

No audio was downloaded, streamed, played, decoded, transcribed or uploaded. No reference corpus/archive was acquired. Only documentation (including short publisher example sentences), public metadata and four audio HEAD requests were retrieved. No accounts, tokens, models, private recordings or application sources were accessed. No builds/tests/linters/formatters were run.

### Reference and coverage limits

The publisher's term “ground truth” distinguishes original audio from synthesized audio; it does **not** establish the displayed text as guaranteed acoustic-verbatim human reference. The document's sentence list and table even differ in punctuation. Treat those strings as publisher-written sentence labels/read-text aids only. The two already selected independent competent human reviewers must create/re-audit actual-speech references from the acquired audio **before scoring**, independently and blind to model output; no listening or audit has happened in this research.

The visible read text includes geographic names and ordinary German words, but no numeric expressions or explicit negation examples. Audible fidelity even for those names remains unaudited. There is no documented spontaneous/code-switching/unclear-span/silence-only/noise-only/long-speech coverage in this four-file choice. It is single-speaker, tiny and intentionally easy/selected demonstration material, not representative sampling. Do not concatenate it and call the result naturally long speech or add silence/noise and call that a spontaneous corpus.

**Do not substitute the 2022.10 ZIP.** [Zenodo record 7265581](https://zenodo.org/records/7265581), version **1.0**, lists 12,450 phrases, over 11 hours, 22,050 Hz mono, amplitude normalization to −24 dB and removed leading/trailing silence. [Its API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/7265581) lists `ThorstenVoice-Dataset_2022.10.zip` as **1,388,437,624 bytes**, MD5 `c2c2cb0d8a2b3b240e140d9213cd39b8`. Importantly, its prose says CC0 while its structured `metadata.license.id` says **`cc-by-4.0`**. This is a real publisher metadata discrepancy; do not silently apply one to the ZIP. CC BY 4.0 permits reuse/redistribution, including commercial, but requires attribution, license link and indication of changes, unlike CC0. [Official CC BY terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). That archive is neither needed nor proposed here. The pinned GitHub samples have their own directly observed CC0 repository provenance.

## 2. FOLK/DGD: strong spontaneous references, restrictive access

### Verified corpus/references

IDS's [FOLK corpus metadata](https://agd.ids-mannheim.de/FOLK_extern.shtml) gives **version 2.26, released 2026-05-06**, 459 conversational events, 1,468 speakers, **401 h 7 min**, 3,734,952 verbal tokens. The [project description](https://agd.ids-mannheim.de/folk.shtml) covers authentic private, institutional and public interactions. It uses modified-orthography **cGAT/GAT2 minimal transcripts**, time-aligned in segments no longer than five seconds, plus separate **orthographic normalization, lemma and POS layers**. These are actual-speech-oriented transcripts, not reading prompts, but normalized layers must not be conflated with literal speech.

Names and identifying place/institution/organization information are pseudonymized in transcripts and replaced with Brownian noise in audio, except public interactions. Masked names are therefore not names one can expect a recognizer to hear; privacy masking is not a naturally unclear/noisy speech example. [FOLK metadata](https://agd.ids-mannheim.de/FOLK_extern.shtml).

The publisher-linked [2011 FOLKER transcription manual](https://agd.ids-mannheim.de/download/FOLKER-transcription_manual.pdf), section 4, documents filled pauses, laughter/breathing/nonverbal notation, uncertainty/alternatives, unintelligible `+++` passages, written-out numbers, lowercase word chains and cliticization. This proves the existence of relevant notation, not that this old manual completely defines release 2.26. The [current conventions index](https://agd.ids-mannheim.de/gat.shtml) points to **cGAT handbook 1.2 (October 2023)** and normalization guidelines **October 2019**. Their linked substantive current documents, [handbook](https://ids-pub.bsz-bw.de/frontdoor/index/index/docId/12186) and [normalization guidelines](https://ids-pub.bsz-bw.de/frontdoor/index/index/docId/9326), presented an Anubis challenge during research; exact current transformations were not inspected. Negation, code-switching language pairs, event-level target coverage and audible references remain selection/audit questions, not established by corpus size.

### Current restrictions and pilot feasibility

The [DGD terms dated 2026-03-19](https://dgd.ids-mannheim.de/dgd/pragdb.dgd_extern.sys_use) require registration, valid email, named institution and stated research/teaching/study purpose. Standard access is for **scientific noncommercial use at universities or comparable scientific institutions**. They prohibit commercial use, third-party transfer, ML/LLM training/finetuning and sending data to online processing services (including browser AI/translation features). Downloaded excerpts have single-copy/no-other-person-access, no-stitching and deletion-when-unneeded restrictions; scientific quotation/publication requires suitable masking, copyright compliance and IDS/DGD/corpus/document citation. This is **not** a Creative Commons redistribution license. FOLK is available **only through DGD**, not the AGD personal-copy service; do not propose paid AGD ordering as a bypass. [FOLK access statement](https://agd.ids-mannheim.de/FOLK_extern.shtml).

[INFERENCE] Offline inference-only testing is not the expressly banned training activity, but that does not establish permission for this product's assessment, commercial purpose, external independent reviewers, sharing references/model outputs or publishing fixtures. Confirm the applicable authorized purpose and reviewer access with IDS before proposing acquisition. Do not create an account, accept terms or send restricted audio to cloud Whisper under the present research authorization.

A [publisher manual preview](https://agd.ids-mannheim.de/download/FOLKER-Transkriptionshandbuch_preview.pdf), p.8 footnote 12, documents maptask event **`FOLK_E_00100`**, transcript **`FOLK_E_00100_SE_01_T_01`**, recording HBG1_HBG2, duration **21:37**. This is an exact **documented candidate identifier**, not verified current full-download eligibility, audio URL or byte size. Selected complete datasets exist, but registration is needed to establish the currently authorized set and paired export formats. **Exact asset URLs/bytes/current eligibility unavailable publicly in this investigation.** No full corpus acquisition is proposed; the excerpt no-stitching rule and publisher-authorized selected complete downloads are distinct.

## 3. Kiel Corpus of Spontaneous Speech: paid personal research resource

The [Kiel publisher page](https://www.isfas.uni-kiel.de/de/linguistik-und-phonetik/forschung/kiel-corpus) identifies the **revised/enlarged 2017 edition**, editors Klaus J. Kohler, Benno Peters and Michel Scheffers. It combines VerbMobil appointment-making and VideoTask dialogues. Button-operated mutually exclusive-channel g-dialogues are explicitly described as unscripted but **not fully spontaneous**; VideoTask and f06 permit overlap. This is not evidence of mixed German/other-language coverage.

[2017 documentation](https://www.isfas.uni-kiel.de/de/linguistik-und-phonetik/forschung/kiel-corpus/docs/Info_KielCorp_2017.pdf), §§1.3 and 4.2, documents **8:34:35** spontaneous speech from **64 speakers**, 16-bit 16-kHz RIFF WAVE. Every spontaneous dialogue has an orthographic `.trl` transliteration produced by advanced phonetics students under supervision. Examples preserve false starts/repetitions and mark breathing, pauses, clicks, hesitation, laughter and throat-clearing. References use pre-reform DUDEN spelling, TeX-style ASCII umlaut/ß escapes, 7-bit ASCII and CRLF, not normalized modern prose. Canonical phonematic transcription generated from the orthographic text is not directly observed phonetic speech; segmental `.s1` and prosodic `.s2` coverage differs by subset. No measured guarantee of verbatim lexical accuracy is given.

Exact publisher-documented 2017 units include `VerbMobil/g07a/g071a/g071a00r.wav` with task `g071a.trl`, overlapping `VerbMobil/f06a/f061a/f061a.wav`, and `VideoTask/l01a/l011a/l011a.wav` with `l011a.trl`. Tiny turn example **`g123a01r` = 3.6495000 s** is documented, but is **not** a public downloadable pilot asset. Old transcript turn numbers and new two-digit/channel-suffixed filenames must not be blindly joined. [Corpus documentation](https://www.isfas.uni-kiel.de/de/linguistik-und-phonetik/forschung/kiel-corpus/docs/Info_KielCorp_2017.pdf).

The [official order form/terms](https://www.isfas.uni-kiel.de/de/linguistik-und-phonetik/forschung/kiel-corpus/kiel-corpus-bestellung) offers the spontaneous section alone for **€600**, on a **mailed USB stick**, with VAT treatment additional/as applicable (not a universal VAT-inclusive quote). Copyright remains; own research use only, no third-party transfer or commercial speech-data use. No verified rights for external reviewer sharing, cloud processing, published audio fixtures or derived-output distribution were established. No per-turn purchase/free pilot/download endpoint or publisher distribution byte count was found. A small subset could be selected **after authorized full-section acquisition** [INFERENCE], but that is not small acquisition. This source is therefore not recommended as the first approvable pilot.

## 4. CALLHOME German: explicit actual-speech conventions, separate restricted licenses

Publisher catalog **[LDC97S43](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC97S43)**, 1997, describes roughly **48 hours from 100 unscripted native-German telephone conversations**, 8-kHz two-channel μ-law SPHERE audio compressed with SHORTEN. Callers in North America chose family/friends overseas and their own topics; calls lasted up to 30 minutes. This is a particular historical/telephone/expatriate social sample, not modern broad population coverage. Consent at collection is documented in the [publisher transcription description](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/docs/LDC97T15/german_transcript.txt).

Transcripts are a separate product **[LDC97T15](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC97T15)**. Catalog prose mentions five-/ten-minute regions, but the detailed release description says the released 80 training + 20 development calls use **ten-minute regions**; the separately collected five-minute evaluation regions were held back. Do not assume the 48 hours of audio is all transcribed.

The [official conventions/QC document](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/docs/LDC97T15/german_transcript.txt) describes human auditory/visual transcription and subsequent human content passes, often three or more. It retains hesitation nonlexemes (`%`), partial words, interrupted turns and repetitions; marks names (`&`), foreign-language spans (`<language text>`), speaker/background sounds, overlap, uncertainty/best guesses (`((text))`) and fully unintelligible passages (`(( ))`). Numbers are **spelled out**, mispronounced words use usual orthography inside `+…+`, hesitation spellings come from a finite list and punctuation is discretionary. It is an actual-speech human transcript with declared normalization conventions, **not guaranteed ground truth**. References must retain uncertainty rather than force guessed words into exact-match targets; two blind human re-audits remain necessary. No per-asset negation/code-switching coverage was verified.

The linked **[LDC nonmember user agreement](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/license/ldc-non-members-agreement.pdf)** permits only noncommercial linguistic education/research/technology development; commercial product development triggers for-profit membership/fees before release. It forbids redistribution outside the named research group except limited excerpts in research reports/articles, and requires appropriate scholarly reference. [Acquisition policy](https://www.ldc.upenn.edu/language-resources/data/obtaining) requires accounts, signed applicable agreements and fees/payment before processing. Both audio and transcripts require lawful access; external reviewers must fit the licensed group rather than receive unlicensed copies.

The catalog exposes an audio sample link, but its presence is **not** a separate unrestricted license. No audio sample was fetched. Full-package bytes, exact small paired audio/transcript sample identifiers, current fee and current acquisition eligibility were **not established**. Do not guess these or propose a public repo fixture from this source.

## 5. DECM: relevant spontaneous German-English speech, not acquisition-ready

The authors' [2024 DECM paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.400.pdf), §§3, 5–6, describes **3.38 hours of YouTube videos** selected from finance, computer science, gaming and Denglisch-related topics. It uses ASROT-generated initial segmentation/transcripts and corrections, followed by a second manual correction by native German speakers proficient in English. Word-level English/Denglisch labels and true case/punctuation are provided; English-derived German inflections and hyphenated mixed compounds follow editorial policies. A printed example retains a repeated `schlägt`; numbers may be digit-form references (including English-spoken `5`). These facts do not prove consistent filler/repetition/self-correction/unclear-span retention across the corpus. Human correction seeded by ASR also cannot be assumed blind to recognition hypotheses.

The split sizes are low-CSW **401 utterances / 50.68 min**, mid-CSW **553 / 75.59 min**, high-CSW **602 / 78.85 min**. Topic and CSW selection bias matters; German-English evidence would not establish German-Turkish, German-Arabic or other pairs.

The [author repository](https://github.com/enesyugan/DECM) currently lists only `LICENSE` and `README.md`, despite README text referring to a `download-mng` script directory. It requires transcript requests to **enes.ugan@kit.edu** and says YouTube/Google handles data licensing. The repository's MIT designation is **not** verified permission to download/relicense third-party YouTube audio. The paper's CC BY-NC 4.0 is the **paper** license, not an automatically transferable license for every source recording. No per-video permission/CC license, exact audio URLs, byte sizes, downloadable paired pilot or reference access terms were established. **Do not use downloader workarounds or ask for transcripts under this research-only authorization.** DECM is a source to seek explicit permissions from, not a present acquisition choice.

## 6. Spoken Wikipedia/Idiap: lawful mixed-language read-speech alternative, normalized references

The [Idiap dataset page](https://www.idiap.ch/en/dataset/code-switching-speech-corpus) and [Zenodo record 4434251](https://zenodo.org/records/4434251), **v1, 2021-01-12**, describe resegmentation of German Spoken Wikipedia into **34 hours of German-English read speech**. The record lists **12,437** segments; the [paper](https://publications.idiap.ch/attachments/papers/2022/Khosravani_ASRU-2_2021.pdf) says **12,511**. Keep the chosen release's figure; do not merge discrepant counts.

The [record API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/4434251) lists `code-switching.tar.gz` as **1,426,652 bytes**, MD5 `ee84d7577cb7bcb080fae2e0a903b700`, and metadata license **CC BY-SA 3.0**. Exact distribution URL: https://zenodo.org/records/4434251/files/code-switching.tar.gz?download=1 . The publisher describes providing resegmentation means/Kaldi data, **not a 34-hour audio payload in that small archive**. Contents were not acquired or inspected. [Official CC BY-SA 3.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) allow commercial copying/adaptation but require attribution, license link/change indication, share-alike for distributed adaptations and no added legal/technical restrictions. Cite Khosravani/Garner and the SWC source authors; preserve individual source speaker/article provenance if acquiring audio later.

The [original SWC publisher](https://nats.gitlab.io/swc/) identifies its current release as **October 2017**, free license CC BY-SA 4.0, with [release handle](http://hdl.handle.net/11022/0000-0007-C641-0). Its **older Spring 2016** audio archive is separately described as CC BY-SA 3.0 and **11 GB** German audio (rounded publisher figure, not exact bytes). These are distinct releases/licenses; do not infer that one license or archive applies universally to every derivative or original Wikimedia recording. Per-file original rights/attribution must be retained.

Although the Idiap description calls the XML manually annotated, the **original creators' [SWC manuscript](https://arne.chark.eu/static/spoken-wp-corpus-collection.pdf), §§3–4**, explicitly describes an **automatic** normalization/alignment pipeline with **no manual correction of generated corpus data**, except targeted evaluation and source metadata fixes. It aligns written article text, uses MaryTTS number/date/abbreviation normalization and generates spoken header patterns. Readers may omit text or speak words absent from text; misnormalization and incomplete alignment are documented. German alignment covers only part of total speech; unaligned spans cannot be assumed silence or unintelligible. Therefore treat SWC XML as a **normalized article-derived alignment**, not a comprehensive faithful human transcript. This underlying-method evidence overrides any temptation to describe the entire resource as manually verbatim.

Exact small paired mixed-language audio IDs/URLs/bytes were not established without acquiring the reference/segmentation distribution. Original audio sizes and current selective download access remain **unknown** here. This is a lawful control/resource lead, not the proposed four-file pilot and not spontaneous filler/self-correction/unclear-span qualification.

## Coverage and acquisition consequences

| Requirement | Bounded Thorsten control | Gated/restricted spontaneous sources | Remaining evidence gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary short German speech | Four publisher-labeled original recordings; audible text unaudited | FOLK/Kiel/CALLHOME contain short turns | No actual-speech reference audit yet |
| Fillers, repetition, self-correction | Not documented in four samples | FOLK/Kiel/CALLHOME notation is relevant; DECM one repeated-word example | Select and blind-audit actual occurrences; do not normalize them away |
| Names, numbers, negation | Geographic names in read text; numbers/negation absent from displayed examples | CALLHOME names/numbers conventions; FOLK privacy masking; Kiel task context | Exact audible target occurrences and coverage counts unknown |
| Silence/noise and unclear speech | No documented dedicated cases; no listening | CALLHOME unclear/noise notation; FOLK uncertain notation and artificial privacy masking; Kiel nonverbal symbols | No approved silence-only/noise-only or naturally unclear-span pilot; masking is not natural uncertainty |
| Long continuous speech | None documented | Long FOLK events/CALLHOME calls; Kiel turns/dialogues | Acquisition/export restrictions and reference coverage must be settled |
| German/other-language speech | None documented | DECM specifically German-English, with unresolved acquisition rights; CALLHOME foreign-language notation alone is not verified bilingual coverage | No ready licensed spontaneous mixed-language pilot; SWC/Idiap is read speech |
| Regional/dialect fidelity | Not established | No regional dataset survey in this report | Entire dialect qualification remains outside these controls |

**Possible training contamination:** OpenAI's [official Whisper model card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/whisper/main/model-card.md) describes internet audio/transcripts, weak supervision, hallucinations, repetition and uneven accent/dialect performance. The [large-v3 model card](https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3) specifies one million weakly labeled hours plus four million pseudo-labeled hours, but exposes no item-level inclusion/exclusion list for these recordings. The original card's 680,000-hour account describes the earlier training series and must not be used as the large-v3 total. Public historical Thorsten examples, Wikipedia readings and YouTube sources may overlap training [INFERENCE]; neither overlap nor clean holdout status is verified. Gated/purchased corpus access likewise does not prove training absence. Record contamination status as **unknown**, not “unseen.”

A source-specific next acquisition request can conservatively be: **authorize only the four pinned Thorsten original WAVs, 484,236 bytes total, as read-speech controls, with two independent blind human actual-speech reference audits before any scoring**. This request would not authorize the ZIP, models, synthetic examples, reference corpora, private media, online transcription or restricted spontaneous sources. If spontaneous/code-switching behavior is the immediate priority instead, the actionable prerequisite is **publisher permission and reviewer-access clarity** for a named restricted source; this research does not supply that permission.

The source-research deliverable is complete. The ticket's German fidelity/unclear-span behavior is **not qualified** by documentation, HEAD availability or the proposed easy controls. No release criterion, measured model result or broad coverage claim follows from this report.

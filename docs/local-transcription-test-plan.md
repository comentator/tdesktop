# On-device transcription: implementation acceptance plan

For [Decide acceptance criteria and assemble the transcription specification](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/7). This is a **future integrated-client acceptance plan**, not a report of executed tests. Every scenario below is **NOT RUN** until observed against the real implementation. Planning completion does not imply working transcription or shipping readiness.

## Target, authority and evidence boundaries

Target: the current Apple M5 Mac on macOS 27 only; pinned whisper.cpp 1.9.5 with the approved full GGML large-v3 model, Metal preferred and explicit same-model local CPU recovery. No server fallback, smaller-model substitution, translation or cleanup mode. Legacy hosts, shipping signing/notarization certification and a full actual-critical-pressure/reclamation campaign are outside this first local destination.

The authoritative policies are [Decide the local Transcribe interaction and transcript presentation](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/4#issuecomment-6097482730), [Decide model setup and local transcription processing lifecycle](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/5#issuecomment-6097907345), [Decide transcript persistence and deletion boundaries](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/6#issuecomment-6098026550), [Decide German transcript fidelity and dialect handling](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/9#issuecomment-6097655558), and [Decide local transcription policy for protected messages](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/10#issuecomment-6097779917). Later resolutions [Qualify German fidelity and unclear-span behavior](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/12#issuecomment-6103474658) and [Qualify macOS deployment baselines and helper isolation](https://github.com/comentator/tdesktop/issues/13#issuecomment-6103728922) supersede the older quality/platform qualification gates, not their privacy, persistence or lifecycle policies.

### Already observed native basis — not integrated-client acceptance

[Whisper macOS deployment and helper qualification](research/macos-whisper-deployment-qualification.md) records 21 available controls on the existing current-M5 artifact: real Metal and CPU load/RUN of the approved model, restricted selected/managed-file access and unrelated-file/write/loopback denial, UNLOAD/reload, oversized/truncated PCM rejection, isolated native crash/allocation abort, helper reaping, and separate explicit identical-model CPU recovery. These are existing prototype observations, not passes for the matrix below. Registration of pressure notifications with no callbacks did not prove actual pressure delivery or reclamation. Developer-local ad-hoc/runtime signing and restricted Seatbelt execution do not certify shipping App Sandbox, Developer ID or notarization.

The approved model is 3,095,033,483 bytes, SHA-256 `64d182b440b98d5203c4f9bd541544d84c605196c4f7b845dfa11fb23594d1e2`; whisper.cpp commit is `d1be6fde11ac6e0407606b4e42fe72d34add8037`. Native protocol constraints are **one active job client-wide**, **16 kHz float PCM**, and **at most 480,000 samples per window** (30 seconds). This is a PCM-window bound, not a whole-process RAM cap or permission to truncate a message. The observed roughly 134-second CPU Debug zero-PCM RUN is not speech latency. No arbitrary wall-clock, RAM, disk-speed, cancellation-deadline or reclamation guarantee is added here.

### Fidelity risk accepted, not proved

The intended result remains faithful spoken wording, dialect vocabulary/grammar, fillers, repetitions, false starts, corrections, names, numbers, units, negation and language switches, with readable punctuation rather than paraphrase or Standard-German rewriting. Recognition is best-effort: [Whisper German fidelity artifact](research/whisper-german-fidelity.md) records non-speech hallucinations, long-fixture label divergence, incomplete actual-speech references and unqualified genuinely unclear spans. Fluent text is not proof of accuracy; critical meaning may be wrong. Do not invent native confidence percentages or fabricated `[unclear]` labels. Reliable unclear-span behavior, numerical quality thresholds, corpus scoring and broader fidelity qualification are deferred until after the first working implementation; no numerical WER pass, new recordings, live human-review campaign, corpus acquisition or model comparison is required here. Operational boundary checks below are not that deferred quality campaign.

## Execution and result recording

Use the actual Telegram client, native helper, approved model, actual encrypted account storage and normal media/clipboard/export paths. Existing privately held, authorized, eligible voice and video messages are sufficient; no new asset/model download, recording or upload is demanded by this plan. A download scenario exercises approval/acquisition behavior only when acquisition is separately authorized. A private disposable local environment may provide controlled faults at real acquisition, helper or storage boundaries; it must still run the actual consumer path, not substitute a mock transcription echo or assert source-text wiring. Never induce systemwide pressure on the user's active workstation.

For each row record scenario ID, implementation revision, M5/macOS version, action, observed consumer result, content-free supporting evidence and outcome (`NOT RUN`, `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `BLOCKED` with exact prerequisite). Record fault controls as controlled faults, and pressure-policy injection as injection, not real OS-pressure proof. Do not publish raw audio, transcripts, PCM, recognition diagnostics containing content, credentials or user-identifying logs. Count/phase/model/backend/ownership/commit outcomes can provide private or content-free evidence. Consumer-visible results alone do not prove encryption or network denial: corroborate those rows at the real storage/helper boundary without retaining transcript content in diagnostics.

Each row includes its initial status explicitly. Parameterized cases must each be observed; a voice success cannot stand in for video, and one restriction flag cannot stand in for every actual restriction source.

## Minimum actual-client smoke and focused regression boundaries

First working smoke requires actual on-device **voice and video** success on the current M5, not a prototype-only response. Execute U01–U03, U05–U09, M01, M06–M09, L01–L05, L08, D01–D05, R01–R05, P01–P03, P08, P10–P11 and X01–X03 using available approved local prerequisites. Combine compatible rows in one client session rather than inventing a new corpus or repeating acquisitions. Include setup cancel/resume, FIFO/two-account ownership, previous-text preservation, source selection, cancellation/fault/manual CPU recovery, disabled protected actions, known-state offline recognition, restart/cache independence and explicit clipboard behavior. A row needing absent authorized acquisition/fault prerequisite stays NOT RUN or BLOCKED; it is not passed by inspection.

The remaining rows are narrowly focused regressions for implementation boundaries: long preview and PCM tail assembly, model management, pressure-policy handling, remap/unloaded deletion/source change, locks/storage availability, save/delete/corruption races and non-retraction limits. Exercise the relevant rows before claiming those policies work; they are not a demand for legacy/rollout certification or a full pressure/quality campaign. Use controlled disposable-state commit/native faults only where normal client actions cannot exercise the boundary. Do not create permanent test code, download more models/data or impose live-review/corpus acquisition as part of this planning deliverable.

Final evidence must distinguish: planning-approved specification; already observed native controls; real integrated-client scenarios passed; scenarios not yet run/blocked; and expressly deferred fidelity/rollout qualification. No checked planning checkbox or source inspection may substitute for an actual-client acceptance result.
